WHO I AM

I'm Uddhav Shrimali (Neo) — a WordPress and WooCommerce developer with three years of professional experience building custom plugins in PHP 8. I work to WordPress Coding Standards: PSR-4, OOP, clean uninstall, code the next developer can read and maintain. I also built and run ToolNest, a 67-tool web app in Next.js — no signup, no server uploads.

WHAT I DO

Most WordPress problems aren't design problems. They're the moment a client asks for something the page builder can't do — a booking flow with its own rules, a checkout that behaves differently for certain customers, a sync between WordPress and a CRM. That's the work I do. I work white-label with agencies who sell WordPress but have no PHP developer on the team, and directly with WooCommerce store owners who need something specific built or something broken fixed.
